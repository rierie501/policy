Audio/Video Converter Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Audio/Video Converter Android (Android package roman10.media.converterv2) is provided by the independent developer rierie501. Contact rierie501@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or deletion requests.

Media and local processing

The app browses and converts audio and video on your device. It accesses media you select, or your media library when you grant the relevant Android permissions. It stores working copies, media metadata, thumbnails, conversion settings, presets, history and queued work locally. The developer does not operate a media-upload, cloud-conversion or cloud-sync backend. The app does not require a developer account or an app-specific login.

In the recovery release (version 4.0.0 / code 106 and later), selected documents can be copied into private working storage. User-started conversions can continue through an Android foreground service, with notifications and controls where available. You can manage media and notification permissions in Android Settings.

Saving, sharing and backup

Results can be saved to shared device media storage or a folder you choose through Android's document picker. A chosen cloud or network document provider may upload the file and its name under that provider's privacy practices. Sharing passes the selected result to the app or recipient you choose. Providers and recipients control their copies; the developer does not control them.

The recovery release excludes private working media, conversion queues, debug files and cached purchase receipts from Android app backup and device transfer. Older releases, including 3.2.12 / codes 104 and 105, have different storage and backup behavior; Android backups may include app data according to device settings and provider limits. Installing an update does not erase previous backups. Export files you want to keep before uninstalling or clearing app data. Imported originals, public exports, recipient copies and provider backups may remain after local deletion.

Advertising

The app contains Google AdMob advertising. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share approximate location inferred from an IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or other identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. The app does not request GPS location for conversion.

The recovery release requests advertising consent and displays privacy messages where required through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can revisit applicable choices from Settings → Ad privacy choices. Availability and effects depend on your region and Google's consent requirements; declining personalised ads does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing. Android also provides advertising-ID privacy controls. Automatic conversion-start interstitial advertising has been removed in the recovery release; older installed releases may still behave differently.

Analytics and error reports

The recovery release includes Google Analytics for Firebase. Usage analytics are off by default and can be enabled or disabled in Settings. When enabled, analytics may include installation/device identifiers, app interactions, selected format or codec, conversion profile, timing, outcome and purchase-related events. Older releases used analytics without this new opt-in setting. Google may process approximate location and device information as part of these services.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports, non-fatal errors, stack traces, app state, device and operating-system information and installation identifiers for diagnosing failures and improving reliability. Crash reporting is enabled independently of the usage-analytics switch. Failure reports can contain conversion command text, input/output file paths or names, selected codec/profile information and error messages. Although media content is processed locally, do not assume that file names or paths are excluded from diagnostics. Historical releases may use older crash-reporting SDKs.

Premium purchases

Optional premium features use Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment and payment-account information under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer app does not receive your full payment-card details. The app receives purchase status and signed purchase records to verify entitlement, acknowledge purchases and restore premium access. Relevant records are cached locally. Google Play and analytics services may also process purchase-related information according to their settings. There is no separate developer-operated purchase-validation server in the recovery release.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and investigate. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private media unless you intentionally want to share it for support. Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, unless you request earlier deletion, subject to any applicable retention obligation.

To request deletion of support correspondence or other information you believe the developer holds, email rierie501@gmail.com from the address used to contact support, describing the information concerned. The developer will assess what can be identified and removed and explain any limits. A support email address may not identify historical analytics or advertising records. Google Play, advertising services, document providers, recipients and backup providers control their own records; use their privacy controls or contact them where applicable.

Local media and history have no automatic expiry. Use the app's file and history controls, Android storage settings and your chosen document provider to manage them. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private app data but can also remove working media you have not exported. Manage originals, exports and backups separately. There is no app-specific user account to delete, and deleting support correspondence does not cancel premium ownership.

Security and service providers

Android private storage restricts ordinary access by other apps; exported files follow their destination's access controls. The app does not add application-level password protection or encryption to media. Google documents encrypted transport for its current advertising and Firebase services. External document providers, sharing apps, older app versions and their services may have different practices. Providers may process information outside your country.

Descriptions of the recovery release apply when that version is installed; they do not imply that older installed releases have been changed.
